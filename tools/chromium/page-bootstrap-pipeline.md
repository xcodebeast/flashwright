# Page bootstrap pipeline diagnostic

The [Linux diagnostic](../../benchmarks/results/page-bootstrap-pipeline-linux-2026-10-21.json) tests overlapping receipt waits for `Page.enable`, lifecycle enable, `Network.enable`, `Runtime.enable` and `Log.enable`. [The Rust patch](page-bootstrap-pipeline-rust.patch) applies to the private separate-browser baseline reconstructed from [the recovery checkpoint](recovered-linux-2026-10-21/README.md), with the direct-shared candidate reversed as documented there. The browser itself is unchanged.

`FLASHWRIGHT_PIPELINED_PAGE_BOOTSTRAP=1` admits those commands in their original FIFO order before awaiting replies in that order. The default remains serial. Target/session registration, settings, routing, immediate init scripts, auto-attach, frame discovery and renderer-preparation release retain their existing order. Publication still requires every successful reply. `FLASHWRIGHT_PAGE_BOOTSTRAP_AUDIT=1` emits cumulative stage intervals; all four diagnostic captures enable it.

Operation and original-page guards cover both admission and reply waits. On failure, already dispatched commands may execute after their reply handles are dropped. Existing disposal of the original unpublished target owns cleanup; this does not prove acknowledgement of every sibling. The original init-script differential fixture, three selected creation/closure/timeout tests and five root-detachment fixtures pass. Explicit protocol cancellation inside a domain-enable command and a late failed receipt after other commands complete remain gaps.

The diagnostic records 60 fresh contexts, including warm medians over the final fourteen contexts of each capture. Warm page creation improves 19.80% and 20.76% in opposite orders. The predeclared minimum of 20% in both orders is not met. Preserve the approximately 1 ms component saving without claiming complete-suite benefit; no suite timing campaign or public adoption follows. The result does not bound every possible native bootstrap architecture.

Source and the known baseline driver were restored by hash; the candidate binary and raw captures remain in ignored `workbench/page-bootstrap-attribution-001`. Restoration did not rebuild the driver, so intermediate target-directory objects remain candidate objects until the next build. The corrected private build used the ordinary path-remapping wrapper and took 18.89 seconds after the admission guard change. A mistaken initial build from the product directory was interrupted before testing and remains recorded separately.
