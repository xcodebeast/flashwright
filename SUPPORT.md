# Support

- **How do I…?** Flashwright has Playwright's API, so [Playwright's documentation](https://playwright.dev/docs/intro) applies. Flashwright's own options are in the [README](README.md#speed-options).
- **Something breaks or behaves differently with Flashwright:** open an [issue](../../issues/new/choose).
- **The same happens with stock Playwright:** report it to [Playwright](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright/issues).
