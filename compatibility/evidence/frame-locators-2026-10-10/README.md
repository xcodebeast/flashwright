# FrameLocator development evidence

The adjacent checkpoint records 151 passing native macOS arm64/Bun core groups and 212 differential checks. This is functional development evidence, not performance or release acceptance. The export manifest binds every normalized report/log to its original bytes; raw trace ZIPs remain private at their recorded hashes.

`run-trace-verifier-corruption-checks.py` is the unchanged historical harness used for the retained corruption confirmation. Its defaults assume the original private `.cache/frame-locator-001` location. To replay it, supply both `--verifier /path/to/checkout/tools/verify-frame-locator-traces.py` and `--evidence-directory /path/to/evidence-parent`. That evidence parent must contain the original or equivalently regenerated `differential-003` and `differential-004` datasets, including their raw trace ZIPs. Regenerated datasets produce new report/archive identities and a new confirmation; they do not reproduce the recorded historical SHA256 identities. The exported reports alone cannot replay ZIP corruption checks. The fixture and current verifier can regenerate and check current archives separately.

The shared input-retry regression is candidate-only controlled scheduling. It verifies that both attempted input targets remain archived; it does not measure speed or establish matched reference behavior. The pinned viewer selects the first snapshot with the requested name, even when later attempts are retained.
